# Company List

The **Company List** contains all companies defined in the system.

A single SPUMA installation can be used to register documents for multiple companies. Access to companies and their documents is controlled by the permissions mechanism.

Companies belonging to the same organization can share objects such as classes, dictionaries, and schemas.

From the list, you can:

- add a new company,
- delete the selected company,
- delete multiple selected companies at once,
- search for a company,
- sort the company list.

Options for adding and deleting companies are available in the menu in the upper-right corner. Search and sorting controls are available at the bottom of the list.

---

## Company

A **Company** represents an entity managed in the SPUMA system. Multiple companies can be configured within a single installation.

Company configuration defines, among other things:

- basic company data,
- assigned dictionaries,
- supported classes,
- permissions,
- SAP integration,
- Business Partner configuration.
